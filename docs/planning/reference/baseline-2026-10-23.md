philosophy. [PRODUCT_VISION.md](../../PRODUCT_VISION.md) and the roadmap now
make shutdown/restart control, simple Block/Allow and indefinite manual
protection explicit. Future timers and task sessions remain optional; releasing
one cannot cancel manual Block. Global Allow must remain available and cannot
be silently undone by automation. No such future session feature was implemented.

An independent read-only review found no material contradiction in that contract
across the vision, plan and relevant roadmap slices.

## Security and permitted claims

The [security audit](../../SECURITY_AUDIT.md) already marks bounded control reads
resolved. The integration also retains the later status connection-budget and
shutdown-wakeup fixes. These do not constitute hostile-client hardening of every
wait or every IPC endpoint.

Remaining documented debt includes same-session mutex denial of service,
default status-pipe DACL, ordinary-file/hardlink log targeting, effective scheduled
task ACL validation, and ETW naming/buffering. These are not silently closed by
a green test result. In particular, the status-pipe security finding must be
addressed/reviewed before broader distribution under `RM-reference-008`.

Keep claims limited to source-first use on self-administered machines and the
specific tested scenarios. The
[historical idle-power record](../../IDLE_POWER_VALIDATION.md) remains bound to
its original older binary. Physical S3/display, Hibernate and Modern Standby
remain outside this run. The separate passive-kit fixtures do not validate them.
